\section*{Open Science}

We will make the following artifacts available through an anonymous link at submission \pending{anonymous artifact
URL}:
\begin{itemize}
\item the eBPF probe and loader sources, the proof scripts that check one token per system call, and the lineage proof;
\item the token model, the cross-fit and threshold code, and the scorers for LID-DS~2021, LID-DS~2019 and CB-DS;
\item the cluster pipeline (worker, bridge, RCA, campaign driver) and the five attack fixtures;
\item every pre-registered protocol with its amendments, the analysis scripts, the result receipts behind each number
  in this paper, and the audit script that recomputes the reported numbers from those receipts.
\end{itemize}
The public datasets are available from their authors. We cannot share the raw live captures in full (about 140\,GB per
day of token and decision logs, and cluster-specific identifiers); we share the per-trial evidence, the fitted models
and the aggregated receipts instead. Cluster credentials and hostnames are removed.
